Capstone · May 2027

Five minutes. A panel of judges. One idea you've spent a year on.

The Capstone Pitch Event is the finish line of the AI Innovators year. Student teams present the business they built — market evidence, financials, marketing plan, and all — to a panel of outside industry judges who score it and question it in real time.

How you're scored

The rubric, in plain terms.

Problem clarity

Is there a real problem, and can you show someone actually has it?

Market evidence

Do your numbers come from somewhere, and can you name the source?

Financial reasoning

Does the money work, and do you understand your own assumptions?

Responsible AI use

Can you show where AI helped, where you overrode it, and why?

Delivery

Five minutes, clear story, confident under questions.

Team

Does the work hold together, and does everyone know their part of it?

The full rubric is shared with students at the start of Fall, not held back until the end. You should know exactly what you're being measured on.

Judges

Who's in the room.

Industry professionals from entrepreneurship, technology, and small business, recruited through LBCC's industry advisory committees, regional chambers of commerce, and faculty networks.

Judges score against the rubric, give each team direct verbal feedback, and stay afterward for open networking with students.
